# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Standards

Examples of positive behavior:

- respectful disagreement
- constructive PR/issue feedback
- focusing on what is best for maintainers and users

Unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- publishing others' private information
- spam or malicious content in issues/PRs

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the primary maintainer via GitHub (@Eliksey). Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or block accounts as needed.
